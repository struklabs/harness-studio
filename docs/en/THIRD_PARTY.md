## Redistribution, Commercial Use, Embedding, and Hosting

MIT permits commercial use, embedding, modification and redistribution when its copyright/permission notice is retained. Apache-2.0 permits those uses subject to its license, notice, modification and patent conditions. These code licenses do not grant trademark endorsement, model/dataset rights, API-account rights, or additional GPU-library rights. Hosting is still subject to the exact component/service/model terms. A hosted Microsoft Learn MCP connection consumes a service; distributing its MIT configuration does not license the service. Model download metadata must be checked independently (including attribution, noncommercial, gated and acceptable-use terms).

For a release or any switch from on-demand download to bundling: record exact tag/hash, preserve LICENSE/NOTICE and binary third-party notices, review all wheels/libraries in the relevant lock/archive, confirm commercial/embedding/hosting rights for the intended use, and block redistribution of unresolved artifacts. Do not turn an upstream project's MIT label into approval for its entire dependency closure. Runtime archives are extracted with ordinary files retained; local binary paths leave attribution management to that installation. The mutable Hermes installer and registry snapshots need review when updated.
